# Changelog

## 1.0.4 (31 Oct 2026, AEDT) - initial release
- First public version of Ashtonite.CamLink: Center free cam with mouse look and gamepad support, player head link (Stare & follow, First-person, Just behind head), Side cameras linked to spectators with left/right placement, predictive follow, hair mode (spring chain with rigidity falling off down the chain), no-bounce floor rest, Save / Reset settings.
- Status: compiles with luau-compile. **Not yet tested in game.**
